====================================================================
TypeVerse Desktop — Quick Installation Guide (Students & Teachers)
====================================================================

TypeVerse works 100% offline. All your typing speed records, lessons,
profiles, and badges stay saved privately on your own computer.

Because TypeVerse is a free community/classroom app distributed without
a paid commercial code-signing certificate, Windows and macOS will show
a standard "unknown publisher" check the first time you open it.
This is normal for unsigned independent apps — follow the steps below.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
WINDOWS (Windows 10 / Windows 11)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Two versions may be provided:
  • TypeVerse-Setup-x.y.z.exe     (Standard installer — no Admin needed)
  • TypeVerse-Portable-x.y.z.exe  (Runs directly from USB or Downloads)

When you double-click the file:
1. Windows SmartScreen may show a blue window saying:
   "Windows protected your PC — Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented
   an unrecognized app from starting."
2. Click "More info" (under the message text).
3. Click the "Run anyway" button that appears at the bottom.
4. For the Setup installer, pick any folder you like (your Desktop and
   Start Menu shortcuts will be created without Administrator rights).


--------------------------------------------------------------------
macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel Macs)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the downloaded .dmg file and drag TypeVerse into Applications.
2. When you double-click TypeVerse the first time, macOS may say:
   '"TypeVerse" cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified'
   (or "Apple could not verify TypeVerse is free of malware").
3. Click "Cancel" / "Done" on that popup, then open TypeVerse once via:
   - Method A: Open Finder → Applications, hold Control (or right-click)
     on TypeVerse, choose "Open", then click "Open" in the dialog.
   - Method B (macOS Sequoia / newer): Open System Settings →
     Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security section, click
     "Open Anyway" next to TypeVerse, and confirm with your Mac password.
4. After opening it once this way, TypeVerse opens normally every time.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING ON OLDER CLASSROOM COMPUTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
• Screen flickers or looks blank on an older PC?
  Click "Appearance" in the top-right corner of TypeVerse, scroll down
  to "Desktop Safety & Data", turn on:
  "Disable hardware acceleration (use if graphics glitch)"
  and click "Restart now".

• Moving progress to another computer?
  Open "Progress" (or "Appearance → Desktop Safety & Data") and click
  "Export" to save a .json backup file, then "Import" on the new PC.
